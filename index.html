Your First Day

Day one, step by step

Your first day is a calm, structured introduction — no stress, no pressure. Here's exactly what happens.

Contact your moderator

You connect with your personal moderator and agree on the exact date and time of your first shift in advance.

Zoom or Discord call

At the start of the shift, you join a video call with your moderator — in whatever format is most comfortable for you.

Platform training (3–4 hours)

The moderator walks through everything via screen share: how the platforms work, how to automate your workflow, and how to avoid confusion.

Your first live stream (~2 hours)

After training you go live for about 2 hours to practice what you've learned. Minimal audience intentionally — for a comfortable start.

Payment — same day

You receive your payment immediately after the shift. Day one, same day. Every time.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I combine this with studying or another job?
+
Yes — easily. ProfitCast offers a fully flexible schedule. You decide when and how much you work. The minimum is 2 working days per week. Perfect for students or anyone looking for additional income.
Do I need to know English?
+
Not at all. We work through chat using a built-in translator. All processes are automated and simplified. There are zero language barriers.
What if I'm scared to start and have no experience?
+
Completely normal. We support beginners from day one: step-by-step training, constant support from your moderator, a friendly team, and help until you reach real, consistent results. You are never alone in the process.
Do I need to look like a model?
+
No. Live streaming success depends on energy and genuine connection with the audience — not on appearance. Girls with completely different looks and personalities succeed here every day.
What if I don't enjoy it after trying?
+
You lose nothing by trying. You can test it out and decide whether it's for you — no pressure, no obligation. Many girls who were unsure are still with us years later.
How does payment work?
+
You are paid at the end of every shift, the same day. Income comes from three sources: platform monetization, viewer donations, and advertising. Starting from training, you receive payment after every single shift.
